PRACTICE AREAS · CONSUMER MATTERS

Consumer
Matters.

Consumer matters may arise from disputes concerning goods, services, contracts and other transactions between consumers and businesses or service providers.

KEY AREAS
01

Deficiency in Service

Consumer disputes concerning alleged deficiencies in services provided by businesses or service providers.

02

Defective Goods

Proceedings concerning defects or deficiencies in goods supplied to consumers.

03

Unfair Trade Practices

Consumer disputes concerning conduct alleged to constitute an unfair trade practice under applicable law.

04

Consumer Complaints

Complaints concerning consumer transactions and remedies available under the applicable framework.

05

Compensation Claims

Claims for compensation and other relief arising from consumer disputes.

06

Product Liability

Consumer claims concerning liability arising from defective products and related circumstances.

07

Appeals

Appeals and related proceedings arising from orders passed by consumer commissions.

08

Execution

Proceedings concerning enforcement of orders and directions passed in consumer matters.

RELEVANT LEGISLATION

Depending on the nature of the consumer dispute, the applicable legal framework may include:

  • Consumer Protection Act, 2019
  • Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, where applicable
  • Indian Contract Act, 1872, where applicable
  • Sale of Goods Act, 1930, where applicable
  • Other applicable legislation and rules

The applicable framework depends upon the nature of the transaction, the goods or services involved, the parties and the relief sought.

Consumer disputes may also involve contractual, statutory and product-related issues depending upon the circumstances of the matter.

CONSUMER PROCEEDINGS

Consumer
Rights & Remedies.

Consumer proceedings may concern the quality of goods, performance of services, contractual obligations, representations made in connection with a transaction and other matters recognised under applicable law.

The relief available depends upon the facts of the dispute, the applicable statutory framework and the jurisdiction of the relevant consumer commission.

FORUMS

Consumer
Commissions.

Consumer disputes may be presented before the appropriate District, State or National Consumer Commission in accordance with the applicable jurisdictional requirements.

Appeals and other proceedings may arise before the appropriate higher consumer commission or court, depending upon the nature of the order and proceeding.

PRACTICE AREAS · COMMERCIAL DISPUTES

Commercial
Disputes.

Commercial disputes concern rights and obligations arising from business transactions, commercial relationships, contracts and related financial or corporate matters.

KEY AREAS
01

Contractual Disputes

Disputes concerning interpretation, performance, breach and enforcement of commercial contracts.

02

Recovery Claims

Proceedings concerning recovery of money and other legally enforceable commercial claims.

03

Partnership Disputes

Civil disputes arising from partnership relationships, obligations, accounts and related claims.

04

Shareholder Disputes

Disputes concerning rights, obligations and interests arising within corporate or shareholder relationships.

05

Commercial Recovery

Proceedings relating to outstanding commercial dues, invoices, payments and related claims.

06

Business Obligations

Disputes concerning obligations arising from commercial arrangements and business transactions.

07

Commercial Injunctions

Applications seeking interim or permanent injunctive relief in appropriate commercial disputes.

08

Appeals & Revisions

Appellate and revisional proceedings arising from commercial and civil disputes.

RELEVANT LEGISLATION

Depending on the nature of the dispute, applicable legislation may include:

  • Code of Civil Procedure, 1908
  • Indian Contract Act, 1872
  • Specific Relief Act, 1963
  • Limitation Act, 1963
  • Indian Partnership Act, 1932
  • Companies Act, 2013
  • Commercial Courts Act, 2015
  • Other applicable central and state legislation

The applicable procedural and substantive framework depends upon the nature of the transaction, the relief sought, the parties involved and the forum having jurisdiction over the dispute.

Commercial proceedings may also involve contractual dispute-resolution mechanisms, including arbitration, where applicable.

FORUMS

Appropriate
Forums.

Commercial disputes may be instituted or pursued before courts and other forums having jurisdiction over the subject matter and the parties.

Depending upon the nature and value of the dispute, proceedings may arise before the appropriate civil or commercial courts, appellate forums, tribunals or arbitral institutions.

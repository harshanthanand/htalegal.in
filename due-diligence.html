PRACTICE AREAS · DUE DILIGENCE

Legal Due
Diligence.

Due diligence reviews of legal, regulatory, property and commercial matters assist parties in evaluating risk, obligations, liabilities and transaction exposure before concluding agreements or structural decisions.

KEY AREAS
01

Legal Due Diligence

Review of legal status, rights, obligations and material issues affecting a transaction or business.

02

Title & Property Due Diligence

Examination of title documents, encumbrances, registrations and property-related risks.

03

Corporate & Commercial Due Diligence

Review of corporate records, shareholder arrangements, agreements and commercial exposure.

04

Document Review

Scrutiny of contracts, deeds, filings, records and supporting documents for legal and factual issues.

05

Agreement Review

Analysis of transaction documents and associated terms, obligations, liabilities and risk allocation.

06

Transaction Due Diligence

Review of business, commercial and legal considerations relevant to purchase, investment or restructuring matters.

07

Regulatory & Compliance Review

Assessment of statutory compliance, approvals, filings, licensing and applicable regulatory obligations.

08

Due Diligence Reports

Consolidated legal findings, risk summaries and issue matrices supporting informed commercial decisions.

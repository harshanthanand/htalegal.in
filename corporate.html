RELEVANT LEGISLATION

Depending on the nature of the matter, the applicable legal framework may include:

  • Companies Act, 2013
  • Indian Contract Act, 1872
  • Specific Relief Act, 1963, where applicable
  • Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, where applicable
  • Commercial Courts Act, 2015, where applicable
  • Other applicable legislation and regulatory requirements

The applicable legal framework depends upon the nature of the entity, transaction, agreement or commercial relationship involved.

Corporate and commercial matters may also involve regulatory filings, statutory compliances and documentation prescribed by applicable law.

FORUMS

Appropriate
Forums.

Corporate and commercial matters may involve courts, tribunals, regulatory authorities, statutory bodies and other forums having jurisdiction over the matter.

The appropriate forum depends upon the nature of the dispute or application, the parties involved and the applicable statutory framework.

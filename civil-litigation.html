PRACTICE AREAS · CIVIL LITIGATION

Civil
Litigation.

Civil litigation encompasses disputes concerning private rights, obligations, property, contracts and other civil claims before courts and appropriate forums.

KEY AREAS
01

Property Disputes

Disputes concerning title, possession, ownership, boundaries and related property rights.

02

Contractual Disputes

Civil claims arising from contractual rights, obligations, breach and enforcement.

03

Injunctions

Applications for interim and permanent injunctive relief in appropriate civil proceedings.

04

Specific Performance

Proceedings concerning enforcement of contractual obligations through specific relief.

05

Recovery Proceedings

Civil proceedings concerning recovery of money and other legally enforceable claims.

06

Declaration & Possession

Proceedings seeking declaratory relief, possession and related civil remedies.

07

Execution Proceedings

Proceedings relating to enforcement and execution of decrees and orders.

08

Appeals & Revisions

Civil appellate and revisional proceedings before appropriate forums.

RELEVANT LEGISLATION

Applicable
Legal Framework.

Depending on the nature of the dispute, applicable legislation may include:

  • Code of Civil Procedure, 1908
  • Indian Contract Act, 1872
  • Transfer of Property Act, 1882
  • Specific Relief Act, 1963
  • Limitation Act, 1963
  • Registration Act, 1908
  • Other applicable central and state legislation
FORUMS & PROCEEDINGS

Courts &
Appropriate Forums.

Civil matters may arise before courts and forums having jurisdiction over the subject matter, territorial jurisdiction and other applicable requirements.
